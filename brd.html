01

Executive summary

AstroPlanet already offers kundali generation, horoscopes, Panchang, matchmaking, puja bookings and paid chat with astrologers. This program adds an AI guide that reads each user's actual birth chart and answers questions across three domains: core astrology, medical astrology and mental health and wellness.

When a question needs human depth, the guide hands the conversation to a specialist astrologer, chosen by the RUSE ranking system. RUSE scores every astrologer on Reliability, User feedback, Satisfaction and Expertise, weighted differently for each domain, so that people asking about health reach astrologers with proven medical-astrology depth and people in a difficult emotional period reach astrologers whose clients consistently leave feeling better.

Health and emotional wellbeing are sensitive subjects. Safety is therefore a product requirement, not an afterthought: the guide never diagnoses, never replaces a doctor or therapist, and switches to crisis support the moment someone may be at risk.

Three domainsOne AI guide

Chart-grounded answers in English, Hindi and Hinglish, available around the clock.

Human depthSpecialists on demand

One-tap handoff to a matched astrologer, with a consented brief so the user never repeats themselves.

Quality engineRUSE ranking

Rewards astrologers who are reliable, well reviewed, genuinely helpful and verifiably expert.

By designSafe and private

Medical and crisis guardrails, DPDP-aligned consent, and no training on user data without opt-in.

Scope, assumptions & constraints

In scope · Release 1 (public launch, week 16)
  • AI guide across core, medical and wellness astrology on web and mobile web (PWA)
  • English and Hindi, with Hinglish understood and answered naturally
  • Birth chart, divisional charts, dasha and transit computation through the existing kundali service
  • Astrologer discovery, RUSE scoring, tiers and personalised recommendations
  • AI-to-astrologer handoff with a consented context brief
  • Chat and voice consultations with wallet billing (existing flows, extended)
  • Feedback capture, astrologer onboarding and domain certification
  • Trust & Quality console and AI safety review queue
  • Website refresh in the white-and-yellow identity with 3D and motion
Out of scope · or later releases
  • Medical diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions or telemedicine
  • Therapy or counselling services; the platform signposts licensed help only
  • Video consultations (Release 2)
  • Native iOS and Android apps (Release 2; the PWA covers mobile at launch)
  • Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and Marathi (Release 2)
  • Western tropical astrology mode (Release 2 option)
  • Gemstone or remedy e-commerce
Assumptions

The existing Next.js and NestJS platform remains the base. DivineAPI continues to supply chart calculations. Azure remains the cloud. A domain advisory panel is in place by week 2.

Constraints

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules. Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954. Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. A 16-week delivery window.

Dependencies

Access to Claude models through Microsoft Foundry on the client's Azure subscription. DivineAPI availability. Razorpay. Advisory panel time for content review and evaluation.

Moon and mind analysis, emotional patterns, demanding transits such as Sade Sati framed for reflection, mood check-ins, journaling, guided breathing and sleep routines, with crisis signposting built in.

AI guide capabilities

Working name for the guide: Jyoti. It reasons over the user's computed chart, cites its sources and always offers one clear next step.

How the guide behaves

  • Asks for missing birth details and records how certain the birth time is; flags answers that depend on an uncertain time.
  • Explains the reasoning: which planet, house, sign and period lead to the interpretation.
  • Shows the classical or advisor-approved source behind interpretive claims.
  • Speaks warmly and without fatalism. Difficult placements are described as tendencies to work with.
  • Ends with one useful next step: a practice, a question to reflect on, a report or a specialist.
  • Identifies itself as an AI at the start of every conversation.

When the guide suggests a human astrologer

  • The user asks for one.
  • The question spans several charts, for example a family or a business partnership.
  • The guide's confidence is low or the birth time is uncertain and rectification is needed.
  • The user wants rituals or puja, which the existing puja booking flow fulfils.
  • A medical-astrology question goes beyond general wellbeing context.
  • Emotional intensity is high but not a crisis. A crisis goes to helplines, never to an astrologer.

The guide will

  • Frame insights as astrological perspectives for reflection
  • Encourage users to see a doctor or a mental health professional when symptoms or distress come up
  • Suggest only non-invasive remedies: mantra, meditation, charity, routine and lifestyle
  • Show Tele-MANAS (14416) and emergency (112) immediately when someone may be at risk
  • Let users view, export and delete their conversations

The guide will not

  • Diagnose or name a disease, or interpret test results
  • Advise starting, changing or stopping any medicine or treatment
  • Predict death, lifespan, dates of accidents or the sex of an unborn child
  • Guarantee outcomes or present gemstones and remedies as cures
  • Use fear about doshas or transits to sell paid services

Astrologer discovery & RUSE ranking

RUSE turns performance data, user feedback, satisfaction and verified expertise into one explainable score per astrologer per domain. It decides who is recommended, in what order, and who needs coaching.

Search & filters

Domain, specialty, language, price per minute, rating, experience, available now.

Rich profiles

Verified credentials, RUSE tier badge, specialties, reviews, transparent per-minute price.

Recommended for you

Ranked by RUSE, fit with the question and availability, refreshed as the user's needs change.

In-chat handoff

Three matched specialists appear inside the AI conversation when escalation rules trigger.

R

Reliability

Performance metrics

  • On-time session starts
  • Median time to accept a chat request
  • Session completion rate
  • Repeat bookings within 60 days
  • Cancellations, no-shows, disputes (inverse)

Source: session and booking logs

U

User feedback

What users say

  • Star rating, adjusted for review count
  • Review sentiment
  • Complaint rate by category (inverse)
  • Share of reviews from verified sessions

Source: ratings, reviews, complaints

S

Satisfaction

Whether people were helped

  • Post-session CSAT: “Did this help?”
  • Would consult this astrologer again
  • 30-day follow-up: “Did the guidance prove useful?”
  • User retention after the session

Source: in-app surveys and retention

E

Expertise

Verified depth in the domain

  • Verified credentials and years of practice
  • Platform domain certification score
  • Quarterly peer review of consented sessions
  • Specialisation depth in the domain
  • Chart-accuracy audit

Source: onboarding, assessments, panel audits

Scoring rules

# every sub-metric is normalised to 0–100 within its domain cohort RUSE(d) = wR(d)·R + wU(d)·U + wS(d)·S + wE(d)·E RUSE_adj(d) = ( n·RUSE(d) + k·μ(d) ) / ( n + k ) # k = 25 sessions, μ = cohort mean Rank = 0.55·RUSE_adj + 0.30·Match + 0.15·Availability
  • Confidence adjustment. An astrologer with few sessions is pulled toward the cohort average, so one perfect review cannot put a newcomer above someone with 400 strong sessions.
  • Recency. Session-level metrics decay with a 90-day half-life; recent work counts most.
  • Match covers specialty fit to the question, language, price band and the user's past preferences. Availability favours astrologers online now or free within 30 minutes.
  • Exploration. Two of the top ten recommendation slots go to Rising astrologers with an adjusted score of at least 60.
  • Load balance. No astrologer receives more than 15% of recommendation impressions for a topic in any hour.
  • Hard gates. Incomplete verification, an open serious complaint, or a medical-cure or fear-selling violation removes an astrologer from recommendations pending review.
  • Cadence. Scores recompute nightly; complaints and hard gates apply immediately.

The RUSE Matrix

Plotting each astrologer by Expertise against the average of User feedback and Satisfaction gives the Trust & Quality team a single picture of the network. The threshold for both axes is 70.

The matrix turns the score into action. North Stars are featured and invited to mentor. Specialists with deep knowledge but weaker client experience get communication coaching. Popular astrologers without proven depth are asked to certify before they appear in medical or wellness recommendations.

Integrity. Only sessions of five minutes or more can be reviewed. Review bursts and rating rings are detected automatically. Rankings cannot be bought; any future sponsored placement is labelled and never changes a RUSE score. Astrologers see their own dimension scores and can appeal.

11

Compliance, safety & ethics

Data protection

DPDP Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules

Itemised consent notices in English and Hindi, purpose limitation, rights to access, correct and erase, a named grievance officer, breach notification, and accounts limited to adults so no children's data is processed. Health and wellness conversations carry a separate consent.

Advertising & claims

No cure claims, anywhere

The Drugs and Magic Remedies Act prohibits advertising remedies as cures for diseases. This rule applies to marketing, astrologer profiles and AI output alike, and is enforced through the code of conduct, complaint categories and output guardrails.

Consumer protection

Fair pricing, no dark patterns

Per-minute prices shown before sessions, clear refund rules, grievance redressal as required by the E-Commerce Rules, and no fear-based or manipulative prompts in the product or the AI.

AI transparency

Users always know it is an AI

AI disclosure in every conversation, sources on request, a guidance-not-certainty disclaimer, and advisory panel review of every knowledge source the AI can use.
