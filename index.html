02 · Design principles

Five rules every component follows

Calculate, then interpret

Charts come from the ephemeris engine. The model never computes a placement or a date.

Ground every claim

Interpretations come from approved, versioned and citable knowledge, not from the model's memory.

Safety runs first

Guards check every message and every answer. A crisis signal overrides everything else.

Humans for depth

The guide hands off to ranked specialists with a consented brief. Nobody starts from zero.

Measure every change

No prompt, knowledge or model change ships without passing the evaluation gate.

    06 · Fine-tuning strategy

    Ground the model first; fine-tune only if the evidence demands it

    Our recommendation

    No fine-tuning at launch.

    Astrological knowledge changes as the advisory panel reviews it, every chart fact must be exact, and safety rules must be auditable. Retrieval, tools and prompts meet all three needs; trained weights meet none of them well.

    Exact charts come from tools. A fine-tuned model would still be guessing placements; the ephemeris engine never guesses.

    Knowledge stays editable. An approved passage can be added, corrected or withdrawn in minutes and cited in every answer.

    Safety stays inspectable. Policies live in versioned prompts and guard rules that are tested on every release.

    Build the grounded baseline

    Prompts, retrieval and tools on the selected model.

    Evaluate on the golden set

    Quality, groundedness, safety, latency and cost against targets.

    Iterate twice on prompts and retrieval

    Most gaps close here, or by fixing the knowledge base.

    Is the remaining gap about style, format or language consistency?

    Knowledge gaps go back to the corpus. Behaviour gaps can justify a fine-tune.

    Fine-tune a narrow component

    LoRA adapter on an open-weight model, trained on Azure ML, promoted only if it beats the baseline on the same evaluation.

    Candidate A · Phase 3 gate

    Safety and intent classifier

    A small open-weight model trained on about 20,000 labelled messages, if guard latency or cost becomes a bottleneck at scale. Labels come from guard verdicts confirmed by the Trust & Quality team.

    Candidate B · Release 2

    Regional-language voice adapter

    Tone and terminology for Tamil and Telugu, trained on consented, de-identified transcripts from Elite-tier astrologers (RUSE ≥ 85) plus exemplars written by the advisory panel.

    08 · Training & evaluation

    Here “training” means tuning prompts, retrieval and guard thresholds against expert-graded data. The same evaluation suite gates every change and would gate any future fine-tune.

    Every stated placement, period and date checked automatically against the computed chart.

    ≥ 4.2Panel quality score, out of 5

    Advisory panel rubric: accuracy, depth, tone, usefulness.

    ≥ 95%Groundedness

    Interpretive claims supported by a retrieved, approved passage.

    ≥ 98%Crisis detection recall

    Measured on the red-team set and on reviewed live events.

    < 0.1%Unsafe output rate

    Diagnosis, medication or fear-selling content reaching a user.

    ≤ 2.0 sFirst token, p95

    Measured end to end under the week-14 load test.

    09 · RUSE ranking engine

    How the RUSE score is computed

    Reliability, User feedback, Satisfaction and Expertise, weighted per domain and adjusted for confidence. Try it with the calculator below; the profiles are illustrative.

    Session, rating, survey, complaint and assessment events→ Normalise per domain cohort→ Weight R · U · S · E→ Confidence adjustment→ Tier + nightly snapshot→ Ranked with live match and availability
    Domain

    Elite

    Weighted contributions · raw score
    Position in the RUSE Matrix

    Confidence adjustment

    (n·RUSE + 25·μ) ÷ (n + 25) pulls thin records toward the cohort mean μ (72 here), so one perfect review cannot outrank 400 strong sessions.

    Recommendation rank

    0.55·RUSE + 0.30·Match + 0.15·Availability, with two of every top ten slots reserved for Rising astrologers and a 15% hourly impression cap.

    Integrity controls

    Verified-session reviews only, burst and ring detection, immediate hard gates for cure claims or fear-selling, and an audited appeal flow.

    Privacy by construction

    Names, phone numbers and emails are removed before any model call. The model receives chart facts, never identity. User data is not used for training without explicit opt-in.

    Prompt-injection defence

    Retrieved text and user content are treated as data, tools are allow-listed per route, and structured outputs are validated before they are used.

    Scale

    Stateless API instances autoscale; Socket.IO scales out with a Redis adapter; reports run from a queue; prompt and chart caching keep latency and cost down.
